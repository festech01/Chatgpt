Your privacy matters.

Last updated: October 15, 2026

What is saved?

Learning plans, lesson progress, bookmarks, and chat history are saved in your browser's local storage. Right now there are no online user accounts and no cross-device syncing. You can export your saved data from the Saved page. Clearing browser site data may remove your plans.

When you use search

Your search phrases are sent to our server and then to YouTube or Brave Search (when connected), so we can show results. Those services have their own privacy policies.

When you use the AI tutor

Your question, recent chat messages, current lesson, and learning goal are sent to our server and to the AI provider to produce an answer. Avoid entering passwords, financial details or other private information. The app asks the AI provider not to store response data, but we cannot promise that third-party services never process or retain information under their own policies.

Free AI question limits

We limit AI use to five questions a day per browser. A signed, secure browser cookie identifies that browser. A daily question counter is stored in our online database and expires after the daily reset. The cookie contains a random identifier, not your name or password. There is also a site-wide limit to help protect the AI budget. Clearing cookies may reset your browser identifier; limits are not the same as personal accounts.

Advertising

SkillScope has space for advertisements. We plan to use Google AdSense only after the publisher account and website are approved and suitable privacy and consent features are set up. Google may use cookies or similar technology to serve and measure ads, depending on your location and choices. Before ads go live, the site operator must update this notice with contact details and a full explanation of any advertising partners, consent choices and data practices.

How to remove your data

Use Clear chat to remove a conversation from this browser. Remove saved plans on the Saved page. Clearing this website's browser storage removes the remaining locally stored information.

This is an early version. For privacy questions, contact the site operator using contact details that should be added before public launch.
